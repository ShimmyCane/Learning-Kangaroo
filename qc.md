# Learning Kangaroo — Quality check

- [ ] `node tools/check_sheets.js` passes (answers match `calc`, five different choices).
- [ ] Our own question, not copied from a real paper.
- [ ] Read aloud, a child knows what to find. Three lines at most.
- [ ] The picture helps but doesn't give the answer away.
- [ ] Wrong choices are believable (the usual slips: counting one twice, off by one, adding instead of taking away).
- [ ] No answers or hints on the kids' pages.
- [ ] Prints well in black and white, two-sided; nothing heavy that shows through.
- [ ] Points fit: 3 = one step, 4 = two steps or a careful look, 5 = a trick or a pattern.
