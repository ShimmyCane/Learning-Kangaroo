# Learning Kangaroo — lessons

## 2026-11-09 (round 1)

- **Prove the answer, don't trust the letter.** Each question carries `calc`; the checker fails if the letter's choice differs or the answer appears twice.
- **Check the drawing reads as the animal.** The first kangaroo looked like a bird at small sizes. Long ears, a big flat foot and a thick tail fixed it.
- **Footers must fit at print size.** The first footer ran off both edges; keep it under about 90 characters at 17 px.
- **Pictures can give the answer away.** Show one jump on the number line, not all of them.
