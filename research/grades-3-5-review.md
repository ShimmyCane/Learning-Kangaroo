**Date:** 2026-11-10 · Calibrated against the official Math Kangaroo USA sample papers, 2022–2026 (the free samples page: https://mathkangaroo.org/mks/practice/free-question-samples/).

## How the bank is set out

- Grades 1–4: questions 1–8 are 3 points, 9–16 are 4 points, 17–24 are 5 points.
- Grades 5–6: questions 1–10 are 3 points, 11–20 are 4 points, 21–30 are 5 points.
- Each sample year has three sample questions per band (one at each point value). Most answers come from a picture, a constraint, or a short chain of reasoning.

## The questions

### W1 · Grade 3 · Wallaby · Set 1

**W1-1** (3 pts)

Five friends line up for a photo. Ana is first. Ben is right behind Ana. Cara is right behind Ben. Eli is between Cara and Dev. Dev is last. Who is third in line?

A) Cara  B) Eli  C) Dev  D) Ana  E) Ben

*Answer A (() => "Cara"): Ana 1st, Ben 2nd, Cara 3rd, Eli 4th, Dev 5th.*

**W1-2** (4 pts)

Kip has 3 red balls and 4 blue balls. Pip has as many balls as Kip. Bo has twice as many balls as Pip. How many balls does Bo have?

A) 12  B) 14  C) 21  D) 7  E) 10

*Answer B (() => 14): Kip and Pip each have 7 balls. Bo has 2 x 7 = 14.*

**W1-3** (4 pts)

A school trip leaves at 9:00 and takes 2 hours and 45 minutes to reach the park. The group stays at the park for 1 hour. At what time do they leave the park?

A) 11:45  B) 12:15  C) 12:45  D) 1:45  E) 2:45

*Answer C (() => "12:45"): 9:00 + 2:45 = 11:45, then + 1:00 = 12:45.*

**W1-4** (5 pts)

Four kangaroos jump 2, 3, 5 and 6 metres, one distance each. Bo jumps 3 metres. Kip jumps further than Bo. Pip jumps 3 metres further than Ana. Ana does not jump 6 metres. Who jumps 6 metres?

A) Pip  B) Ana  C) Nobody  D) Kip  E) Bo

*Answer D (() => "Kip"): Ana 2 and Pip 5 is the only pair 3 apart from the left-over distances, so Kip gets 6.*

### W2 · Grade 3 · Wallaby · Set 2

**W2-1** (3 pts) · picture: sequence

What comes next in the row?

A) heart  B) square  C) triangle  D) circle  E) star

*Answer E (() => "star"): The pattern repeats every 3: star, circle, circle.*

**W2-2** (4 pts) · picture: squares

How many squares of any size are in this picture?

A) 14  B) 16  C) 9  D) 12  E) 13

*Answer A (() => 14): 9 small squares, 4 medium (2 by 2) and 1 big one: 14.*

**W2-3** (4 pts)

Mia has 4 more stickers than Leo. Leo has 3 fewer stickers than Ned. Ned has 10 stickers. How many stickers do Mia and Leo have together?

A) 17  B) 18  C) 21  D) 25  E) 14

*Answer B (() => 18): Leo has 7, so Mia has 11. 11 + 7 = 18.*

**W2-4** (5 pts)

Ana, Ben and Cara each have one pet: a cat, a dog or a rabbit. Ben does not have the cat or the rabbit. Cara has the cat. Who has the rabbit?

A) Nobody  B) Can't tell  C) Ana  D) Ben  E) Cara

*Answer C (() => "Ana"): Ben has the dog and Cara has the cat, so Ana has the rabbit.*

### W3 · Grade 3 · Wallaby · Set 3

**W3-1** (3 pts)

A pencil costs 3 coins and a rubber costs 2 coins. Kip buys one pencil and two rubbers. How many coins does he pay?

A) 9  B) 5  C) 6  D) 7  E) 8

*Answer D (() => 7): 3 + 2 + 2 = 7 coins.*

**W3-2** (4 pts)

Ana counts in 4s from 0: 4, 8, 12, ... Bo counts in 3s from 0: 3, 6, 9, ... Which number less than 20 do they both say?

A) 15  B) 16  C) 6  D) 9  E) 12

*Answer E (() => 12): The numbers in both lists are multiples of 12: 12 (and 24, which is too big).*

**W3-3** (4 pts)

Kip starts at 0 and jumps forward 4 steps three times. Then he jumps back 2 steps. Where is he now?

A) 10  B) 12  C) 14  D) 6  E) 8

*Answer A (() => 10): 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, then 12 - 2 = 10.*

**W3-4** (5 pts)

Bo has some marbles. If he gives 4 marbles to Ana, he will have as many as Ana has now. Ana has 3 more marbles than Cara. Cara has 6. How many marbles does Bo have?

A) 11  B) 13  C) 15  D) 17  E) 9

*Answer B (() => 13): Ana has 6 + 3 = 9. Bo has 9 + 4 = 13.*

### W4 · Grade 3 · Wallaby · Set 4

**W4-1** (3 pts) · picture: missing

Which number is missing?

A) 30  B) 18  C) 20  D) 21  E) 22

*Answer C (() => 20): Counting up by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25.*

**W4-2** (4 pts)

A bag holds 6 apples. Kip has 4 bags and eats 5 apples. How many apples does he have left?

A) 29  B) 17  C) 18  D) 19  E) 20

*Answer D (() => 19): 4 bags hold 24 apples. 24 - 5 = 19.*

**W4-3** (4 pts) · picture: cupcakes

There are 9 cupcakes and 3 of them have an X. Ana eats 2 of the cupcakes without an X. How many cupcakes without an X are left?

A) 5  B) 6  C) 2  D) 3  E) 4

*Answer E (() => 4): 9 cupcakes, 3 with an X: 6 without. 6 - 2 = 4.*

**W4-4** (5 pts)

Five kangaroos stand in a row. Ana is first. Pip is 2 places behind Ana. Bo is directly in front of Pip. Kip is 2 places behind Bo. Cara is last. Who is in the middle?

A) Pip  B) Kip  C) Cara  D) Ana  E) Bo

*Answer A (() => "Pip"): Ana 1st, Bo 2nd, Pip 3rd, Kip 4th, Cara 5th.*

### F1 · Grade 4 · Flyer · Set 1

**F1-1** (3 pts)

12 children like apples and 8 children like pears. 5 children like both fruits. How many children like only one of the two fruits?

A) 7  B) 10  C) 13  D) 15  E) 20

*Answer B (() => 10): Only apples: 12 - 5 = 7. Only pears: 8 - 5 = 3. 7 + 3 = 10.*

**F1-2** (4 pts)

Kip, Bo and Pip share 42 stickers. Kip gets twice as many as Bo. Pip gets 6 more than Bo. How many stickers does Kip get?

A) 12  B) 15  C) 18  D) 21  E) 24

*Answer C (() => 18): Bo = x, Kip = 2x, Pip = x + 6: 4x + 6 = 42, so x = 9 and Kip has 18.*

**F1-3** (4 pts)

A film starts at 2:35 pm and ends at 4:10 pm. How long is the film?

A) 2 h 25 min  B) 1 h 15 min  C) 1 h 25 min  D) 1 h 35 min  E) 1 h 45 min

*Answer D (() => "1 h 35 min"): 2:35 to 3:35 is 1 hour. 3:35 to 4:10 is 35 minutes.*

**F1-4** (5 pts)

Ana, Ben, Cara and Dev each have a different number of marbles from 2 to 5. Ana has more than Ben. Cara has 2 more than Dev. Ben has 3. How many marbles does Cara have?

A) 5  B) 6  C) 2  D) 3  E) 4

*Answer E (() => 4): Ben has 3, so Dev has 2 and Cara has 4. Ana has 5.*

### F2 · Grade 4 · Flyer · Set 2

**F2-1** (3 pts)

Kip is 4th from the front of a line and 7th from the back. How many kangaroos are in the line?

A) 10  B) 11  C) 12  D) 13  E) 9

*Answer A (() => 10): 4 + 7 - 1 = 10, because Kip is counted in both.*

**F2-2** (4 pts) · picture: cells

How many small squares are in this shape?

A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10  E) 12

*Answer B (() => 8): A 3 by 3 square with the middle square missing: 8.*

**F2-3** (4 pts)

Three numbers add up to 30. The first number is twice the second. The third number is 6 more than the second. What is the first number?

A) 6  B) 10  C) 12  D) 14  E) 18

*Answer C (() => 12): Second = x: x + 2x + (x + 6) = 30, so x = 6 and the first is 12.*

**F2-4** (5 pts)

Mia has twice as many pencils as Ned. Ned has 4 pencils. Leo has more pencils than Ned. Mia and Leo have 13 pencils together. How many pencils does Leo have?

A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 5  E) 6

*Answer D (() => 5): Mia has 8, so Leo has 13 - 8 = 5, which is more than Ned's 4.*

### F3 · Grade 4 · Flyer · Set 3

**F3-1** (3 pts)

A pizza is cut into 8 equal slices. Ana eats 3 slices and Bo eats 2 slices. How many slices are left?

A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 2  E) 3

*Answer E (() => 3): 8 - 3 - 2 = 3.*

**F3-2** (4 pts)

How many 2-digit numbers have a 7 in the tens place?

A) 10  B) 11  C) 20  D) 7  E) 9

*Answer A (() => 10): 70, 71, 72, ..., 79: ten numbers.*

**F3-3** (4 pts)

Bo has 3 packets of stickers with 12 stickers in each. He gives 6 stickers to Ana and shares the rest equally between Ben and Cara. How many stickers does Ben get?

A) 13  B) 15  C) 18  D) 10  E) 12

*Answer B (() => 15): 36 - 6 = 30, and 30 shared equally gives 15 each.*

**F3-4** (5 pts)

Ana has 5 more stickers than Bo. Bo has as many as Cara. Cara has half as many as Dev. Dev has 12. How many stickers do Ana and Bo have together?

A) 11  B) 14  C) 17  D) 18  E) 23

*Answer C (() => 17): Cara 6, Bo 6, Ana 11: 11 + 6 = 17.*

### F4 · Grade 4 · Flyer · Set 4

**F4-1** (3 pts)

Using the digits 7, 4 and 9, each once, what is the largest even number you can make?

A) 497  B) 749  C) 794  D) 974  E) 479

*Answer D (() => 974): An even number must end in 4, so the largest is 974.*

**F4-2** (4 pts) · picture: farm

How many legs are there on the farm?

A) 18  B) 20  C) 12  D) 14  E) 16

*Answer E (() => 16): 4 hens x 2 legs = 8. 2 dogs x 4 legs = 8. 8 + 8 = 16.*

**F4-3** (4 pts) · picture: dice

Two dice show 4 and 6, as in the picture. A third die is rolled. All three dice together add up to 15. What does the third die show?

A) 5  B) 6  C) 9  D) 3  E) 4

*Answer A (() => 5): 4 + 6 = 10, so the third die shows 5.*

**F4-4** (5 pts)

A number is multiplied by 3, then 5 is added. The result is 26. What was the number?

A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9  E) 5

*Answer B (() => 7): 26 - 5 = 21, and 21 divided by 3 is 7.*

### B1 · Grade 5 · Boomer · Set 1

**B1-1** (3 pts)

Kip, Bo and Pip each have a different number of pencils: 4, 6 and 9. Kip has fewer pencils than Pip. Bo has more pencils than Kip. How many pencils does Pip have?

A) 6  B) 7  C) 9  D) 12  E) 4

*Answer C (() => 9): Kip < Bo < Pip, so Kip has 4, Bo has 6 and Pip has 9.*

**B1-2** (4 pts)

A 3-digit number uses the digits 2, 5 and 8, each once. The number is odd and bigger than 500. Which is it?

A) 285  B) 528  C) 582  D) 825  E) 852

*Answer D (() => 825): Odd means it ends in 5, and bigger than 500 means it starts with 5 or 8. Only 825 fits.*

**B1-3** (4 pts)

A train leaves at 7:40. Each trip takes 55 minutes. A second train leaves 25 minutes after the first. At what time does the second train arrive?

A) 9:05  B) 9:20  C) 8:20  D) 8:55  E) 9:00

*Answer E (() => "9:00"): The second train leaves at 8:05 and arrives 55 minutes later, at 9:00.*

**B1-4** (5 pts)

Five friends (Ana, Ben, Cara, Dev and Eli) each eat a different number of apples, from 1 to 5. Ben eats 2 and Dev eats 1. Ana eats more than Ben. Cara eats 2 more than Dev. Eli eats 2 more than Ben. How many apples does Ana eat?

A) 5  B) 6  C) 1  D) 3  E) 4

*Answer A (() => 5): Dev 1, Ben 2, Cara 3, Eli 4, so Ana eats the last number, 5.*

### B2 · Grade 5 · Boomer · Set 2

**B2-1** (3 pts)

Bo has 3 boxes. Each box holds 4 cubes. He takes 5 cubes out of the boxes. How many cubes are left?

A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9  E) 5

*Answer B (() => 7): 3 x 4 = 12, and 12 - 5 = 7.*

**B2-2** (4 pts) · picture: cells

How many small squares are in this staircase shape?

A) 8  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6  E) 7

*Answer C (() => 5): Count the squares: 5.*

**B2-3** (4 pts)

Ana, Ben and Cara share 60 marbles. Ana gets 3 times as many as Ben. Cara gets 10 more than Ben. How many marbles does Ana get?

A) 10  B) 20  C) 25  D) 30  E) 40

*Answer D (() => 30): Ben = x, Ana = 3x, Cara = x + 10: 5x + 10 = 60, so x = 10 and Ana has 30.*

**B2-4** (5 pts)

A 2-digit number has a tens digit that is 3 more than its units digit. The two digits add up to 9. What is the number?

A) 72  B) 36  C) 45  D) 54  E) 63

*Answer E (() => 63): Units u, tens u + 3: 2u + 3 = 9, so u = 3 and the number is 63.*

### B3 · Grade 5 · Boomer · Set 3

**B3-1** (3 pts)

A rectangle has a perimeter of 20 cm and a length of 6 cm. What is its width?

A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 7  E) 3

*Answer A (() => 4): Half the perimeter is 10 cm, so the width is 10 - 6 = 4 cm.*

**B3-2** (4 pts)

Dev walks 4 km a day for 5 days, rests for 2 days, then walks 3 km a day for 2 days. How many kilometres does he walk in total?

A) 23  B) 26  C) 29  D) 32  E) 20

*Answer B (() => 26): 5 x 4 = 20 and 2 x 3 = 6. 20 + 6 = 26.*

**B3-3** (4 pts)

Ana has 3 times as many stamps as Ben. Ana gives 10 stamps to Ben, and then they have the same number. How many stamps did Ben have at first?

A) 5  B) 8  C) 10  D) 12  E) 15

*Answer C (() => 10): Ben = b, Ana = 3b. 3b - 10 = b + 10, so b = 10.*

**B3-4** (5 pts)

A bag has only red, blue and green balls. There are 16 balls in all. There are 4 more blue balls than green balls, and twice as many red balls as blue balls. There is 1 green ball. How many red balls are there?

A) 14  B) 6  C) 8  D) 10  E) 12

*Answer D (() => 10): Green 1, blue 5, so red is 2 x 5 = 10. 1 + 5 + 10 = 16.*

### B4 · Grade 5 · Boomer · Set 4

**B4-1** (3 pts)

What comes next in the pattern? 1, 4, 9, 16, __

A) 36  B) 20  C) 21  D) 24  E) 25

*Answer E (() => 25): 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 are 1², 2², 3², 4², 5².*

**B4-2** (4 pts)

Kip has 4 packs of 6 pencils. He gives 3 pencils to each of 4 friends. How many pencils are left?

A) 12  B) 15  C) 6  D) 8  E) 9

*Answer A (() => 12): 24 pencils in all. 4 friends x 3 = 12 given away. 24 - 12 = 12.*

**B4-3** (4 pts)

The average of three numbers is 8. Two of the numbers are 6 and 9. What is the third number?

A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 5  E) 7

*Answer B (() => 9): The three numbers add up to 3 x 8 = 24. 24 - 6 - 9 = 9.*

**B4-4** (5 pts)

Pens come in packs of 3 that cost 10 coins each pack. Ana has 25 coins. She buys as many whole packs as she can. How many coins does she have left?

A) 0  B) 3  C) 5  D) 10  E) 15

*Answer C (() => 5): 2 packs cost 20 coins, leaving 5.*
