# Pouch: logic sheets toward Lean

Six sheets, 24 questions, track **Pouch** (code `P`), in three levels that follow the stages of the lesson plan.

| Level | Sheets | Skill | Lean link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logic 1 (about ages 5–7) | P1 Words that join · P2 If and then | "and", "or", "not", if–then, a rule that breaks | the vocabulary |
| Logic 2 (about ages 7–9) | P3 Find the exception · P4 Chains and proofs | counterexamples, converse, chains, testing a rule | `intro`, testing a claim |
| Logic 3 (about ages 9–11) | P5 Proof steps · P6 Lean-style thinking | "let n be any number", a proof step with a reason, finding the mistake, goals | the goal and tactics |

Each question has one answer that a child can justify in one line (the `why`). Every answer is checked by `tools/check_sheets.js`.

Not yet done: the Lean game itself, pictures of proofs, and printed trials. Ages are a guide, not a label for the child.
