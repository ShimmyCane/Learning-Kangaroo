This page is separate from the Joey and Roo worksheets. Our own problems, written for presenting: read it, work it out, then explain it out loud. The worked steps come from the Roman Numerals tool, which uses a teaching model (split a numeral into its pieces, cross out, merge the rest). It is not a documented Roman method. The front of each sheet has no answer. The back has the answer, the steps two ways and the presenting notes, so keep it for the presenter.

🦘 The ten to present

The most interesting of the 25: messy to start, tidy at the end. Lots of merges, some splits, a few that make you smile.

🖍️ How to practise

  1. Read the front. Guess the answer and how many splits and merges you expect.
  2. Work it out on the front. Then check the back.
  3. Present it to a wall, a toy, then a person. Use the notes on the back: open with the hook, slow down at the hard part, say why it is fun.
  4. Voice: loud enough for the back of the room. Pace: one idea at a time, a pause after each merge. Eyes: look up between steps. Hands: point at the letters you split, cross out or merge.
  5. Two ways: present Way 1 (split, cross out, merge), then Way 2 (by column) and say which one you like better, and why.
  6. Tick the practice tracker on the back after each take.
