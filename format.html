Learning Kangaroo How to add questions · for grown-ups and for Gemini

Every worksheet is one sheet of paper: 4 questions, 2 on the front and 2 on the back. The JSON below is the shape each sheet must follow. Paste it into a chat with Gemini (or any helper) and ask for new questions in the same shape. The file is also data/question-format.json.

Rules

  1. Our own questions, in the contest style: a picture, a short story, five choices. Never copy a real Math Kangaroo question.
  2. Four questions a sheet, with points 3, 3, 4, 5 or 3, 4, 4, 5. 3 = one step, 4 = two steps or a careful look, 5 = a trick or a pattern.
  3. Question text at most 3 short lines (about 130 characters), read aloud a child should know what to find.
  4. Five different choices. Make wrong choices believable: the usual slips (counting one twice, off by one, adding instead of taking away).
  5. The answer letter is A–E. calc is the worked-out answer; the checker proves the letter's choice equals it.
  6. A picture from the list below, or null for text only. It helps but does not give the answer away (show one jump, not all of them).
  7. Level: k for Joey (kindergarten), g2 for Roo (grade 2). tk is the round 1 set behind Settings; do not add to it.
  8. Codes and ids: code = track letter + number (J4, R7). Question id = code + "-" + 1 to 4. Never reuse one. Retire a replaced question with retired: true.
  9. No answers or hints on the kids' pages. The why line is for the answer key only.
  10. No names or ages of the children. Kip, Bo and Pip are our kangaroos.

Choices

Each choice is a number (5), a word ("triangle"), a shape ({"shape": "star"}), a dot-group ({"dots": 6}), or a letter in the picture ({"see": "C"}). Shapes: circle, square, triangle, oval, pentagon, star, heart.
